# Misosofi Practice — roadmap (Atsuko's ideas, keep and remind her)

## Where this app sits
- Three apps: **Piano Course app** (young children / intro), **Misosofi app** (perfect pitch, members), **this Practice app** (the last stage, ongoing lesson students).
- Avoid the same content twice for people who have two apps.
- Practice app: free download; paid tier = theory videos + correction (添削). Existing students: everything unlocked.
- Also to build: a Piano Course app used together with Misosofi — **in a separate chat**.

## Business model (Nov 2026)
- Lesson 70€/h. One lesson a month replaced by the app (theory mostly in the app).
- App with correction: 50€/month existing students, 100€/month new students, members-site-only higher.
- Set correction limits (e.g. videos per month). Later: assistant teachers check with her checklist; she reviews their corrections.
- Website: video-correction sign-up page (like https://www.asahi-net.or.jp/~nt4s-ab/videolesson.html).

## Theory plan
- Lessons give the "dots"; theory videos (hybrid lessons) connect the dots; then "do page N of the theory book" at home.
- Collect theory videos in lessons now; assign book pages once enough dots exist.
- Own theory book covering all ABRSM theory (print + PDF) + correction in the app, aimed at passing real exams. **After monetisation.**

## Home review of lesson work (from her lesson notes, 7 Nov 2026)
- Note Rush levels; rhythm: copy → apple → write (done: Rhythm school v1.37);
- Solfège with rhythms, chromatic up/down, 2-bar phrases;
- Finger training "Remember me", sing RH fingering; hands together two lines;
- Harmony: common note; perfect pitch;
- Circle of fifths, key signatures sung (Fi Di Si Ri Li…), A major sing/play;
- Dotted rhythms, ♪.♬, triplets, quarter divided into 3/4.
